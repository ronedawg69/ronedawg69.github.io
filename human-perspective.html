Working prototype. The 41 events come from the research dataset (core list), edited for length and framing. Sources have been partly checked but not yet fully verified. Marker rule: ring shape shows what an event is (single ring for a capability, double ring for a consequence); colour shows what mainly followed (blue helped, rust harmed, split both). These are editorial judgements, and the cards explain who was helped and who was harmed. Dates are approximate and may mark earliest known evidence, a documented breakthrough, or the start of a wider spread; each marker sits at the start of its date range. Generation rule: every dot or skull drawn is exactly one 25-year generation. Every view is linear: the zoom stops change how much time you see, never how it is spaced.
